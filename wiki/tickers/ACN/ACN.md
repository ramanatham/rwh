## Business Overview

Accenture is the world's largest professional-services firm by revenue (FY25 $69.7B; ≈786,000 employees as of Q2 FY26), providing strategy, consulting, technology, operations, Song (marketing/experience), and Industry X (engineering/manufacturing) services to the world's largest enterprises and governments. It serves 9,000+ clients including ≈75% of the Fortune Global 500, and has partnered with 195 of its top 200 clients for 10+ years. The model is structurally capital-light: capex < $0.7B/year against ≈$11B+ operating cash flow, compounding through revenue growth, margin expansion, buybacks, and dividends.

Effective September 1, 2025, Accenture consolidated delivery into a single **Reinvention Services** unit and reports through three geographic markets (Americas, EMEA, Asia Pacific) and five industry groups (Communications/Media/Tech, Financial Services, Health & Public Service, Products, Resources). Strategic vision: "reinvention partner of choice" in the age of AI, funded by ≈$3B of multi-year GenAI investment and ≈$5B of FY26 acquisitions.

## Pivotal Investment Question

> **Does generative AI structurally shrink the consulting/IT-services revenue pool by collapsing the labor-arbitrage and staff-augmentation model — or does Accenture's scale, ecosystem partnerships, and reinvention positioning let it monetize AI as a *net new* spend category faster than it cannibalizes legacy work?**

The market is pricing the first outcome (multiple at a multi-year trough). The bookings, share-gain, AI-revenue, and ecosystem data so far support the second — but the headline growth deceleration is real and the debate is genuinely open.

## Section 3 — Competitive Moat & Landscape

**Moat: Wide.**

Moat sources:
- **Switching costs / embedded relationships**: 195 of top 200 clients retained 10+ years; multi-year managed-services contracts and deep systems integration make rip-and-replace costly ([FY25 letter](../../../raw/ACN/shareholder-letters/2025_letter.pdf)).
- **Scale / breadth**: ≈786,000 people across 52 countries; 74 $100M+ client bookings in H1 FY26; the only player able to staff multi-service, multi-geography reinvention at scale.
- **Ecosystem intangibles**: top-tier partner with all major hyperscalers/software vendors (AWS, Azure, Google, SAP, ServiceNow, NVIDIA) and now OpenAI (via Accenture Federal partnership, May 2026) — partner-sourced revenue outgrows the firm; FedRAMP-aligned OpenAI capability deepens moat in mission-critical federal deployments.
- **Reinvestment flywheel**: ≈$3B GenAI investment, ≈$0.8B R&D, ≈$1.0B L&D annually, ≈$5B/yr acquisitions — funded internally at high ROIC.

Where the moat faces pressure: generative AI compresses the value of *headcount-based* delivery. If clients self-serve transformation with AI tooling, consulting value-capture could structurally shrink — the thesis-defining vulnerability and the de-rating driver. Honest tail-risk: if AI commoditizes the *integration* layer (not just the labor layer), scale-and-breadth converts from moat into cost base — Accenture's defense is that AI makes orchestration *more* complex, but this must be validated over the next 4–8 quarters.
