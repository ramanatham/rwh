## Business Overview

eBay (NASDAQ: EBAY) is a global online marketplace connecting buyers and sellers across used, refurbished, vintage, collectible, and authenticated luxury inventory — the segments least competed by Amazon's first-party retail or Shopify DTC. The business reaccelerated from a multi-year structural decline (2018–2023) via a strategic pivot into **Focus Categories**: trading cards (organic + Goldin Auctions), motors parts & accessories, luxury (handbags, watches, sneakers via Authenticity Guarantee), refurbished electronics, and pre-owned apparel (Depop). **FY2025 revenue $11.1B (+8% YoY) on GMV ≈$80B (+7%)** — strongest growth since the 2021 pandemic comparison — with Focus Categories now ≈30% of GMV and growing faster than the marketplace ([10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1065088/000106508826000027/ebay-20251231.htm)). The founding insight (Pierre Omidyar, 1995): online auctions create perfect-information markets for hard-to-value, one-off items — trust mechanisms + authentication infrastructure dissolve the C2C friction problem. That insight, refocused, is exactly what's driving reacceleration.

Capital return is aggressive and disciplined: **$2B incremental buyback authorization** + dividend +7% to $0.31/quarter ($756M returned in Q4 2025 alone); share count reduced ≈680M → 448M (–34% in 6 years).

## Pivotal Investment Question

> Has eBay successfully pivoted from a declining horizontal e-commerce site into a structurally-growing **focus-category + circular-economy** marketplace, justifying an inflection re-rate from the historical 11–13× P/E to 16–20× — *or* will **agentic-commerce / LLM-driven shopping disintermediation** (cited as a new risk in the FY2025 10-K for the first time) erode long-tail discovery faster than focus categories can offset, returning eBay to LSD growth?

Bull: (a) Focus Categories sustain >MSD growth and mix gains, (b) advertising / promoted-listings continues scaling as the highest-margin layer, (c) AI shopping-agent pilots evolve into defensible distribution. Bear: agentic commerce (Perplexity, ChatGPT shopping, Amazon Rufus) bypasses eBay's discovery layer while Vinted, Whatnot, and Fanatics fragment the highest-value resale verticals.

**Rating: Narrow (widening in focus categories).**

Network effects in non-new-in-box, collectibles, vehicles parts, and authenticated luxury are real and durable. The horizontal long-tail marketplace is contested by Amazon, Shopify DTC, and — newly — agentic-commerce LLMs.

### Competitive Landscape

| Peer | Scale | Moat / Threat |
|---|---|---|
| **Amazon** | ≈$700B+ GMV | New-in-box dominance; Rufus AI agent encroaches on long-tail discovery |
| **Vinted** (private, EU) | ≈$10B+ GMV; 80M users | Zero-fee buyer-pays model; fastest-growing EU C2C fashion; direct threat |
| **Whatnot** (private) | ≈$8B GMV (+167% YoY); 60% NA+EU live-commerce share | Attacks cards/collectibles/sneakers — eBay's highest-margin verticals — with live-shopping UX |
| **Fanatics** (private) | Owns Topps; multi-billion sports licensing | Vertically integrates cards licensing into a marketplace eBay doesn't own |
| **StockX / GOAT** | 60M+ lifetime StockX trades | Sneaker/streetwear authentication verticals |
| **Etsy** | ≈$13B GMV | Vintage overlap; slower growth |
| **Facebook Marketplace** | Very large, unmonetized | Free C2C floor; structural pricing pressure |

**Differentiators**: breadth × authentication is eBay's edge — no other platform combines 2.5B authenticated-where-needed listings across cards + luxury + vehicles + apparel + electronics. Evidence: active buyers inflected positive in FY25 despite 5+ aggressive vertical entrants. Vehicles P&A is structurally moated (fitment-specific long-tail, Amazon-resistant). Authentication-as-a-service compounds: each new vertical added makes the next cheaper.

**Honest tail risk**: Whatnot is the most worrying — well-capitalized, growing >150%/yr, attacking highest-margin eBay verticals with a live-shopping UX that eBay Live has not yet matched at scale. Fanatics + Topps is the second-most-worrying. Agentic commerce is the wildcard — if LLM shopping agents become primary discovery for long-tail, eBay's catalog becomes infrastructure (low-margin) rather than destination (high-margin).

## Section 7 — Industry-Specific Macro Analysis

**TAM**: Global e-commerce ≈$6T+; non-new-in-box / resale / collectibles ≈$200–450B near-term; expanding with circular-economy and Gen Z preferences. eBay's ≈$80B GMV captures meaningful share of the focused subset ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/topics/10355/c2c-e-commerce/)).

**Structural tailwinds**: Resale / circular economy is durable (environmental, generational, value-driven). Collectibles stabilized at elevated post-2020 volumes. Authentication-as-a-service is the recurring competitive battlefield.

**Agentic commerce** is the genuinely new 2025–2026 structural shift: LLM shopping agents (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Rufus, Google AI Mode) may evolve from product research → autonomous transactions, potentially restructuring marketplace economics around inventory-API access rather than consumer-facing destinations.

**Regulatory**: De minimis tariff exemption ended for US imports (all parcels Aug 2025); EU July 2026; UK to follow. DSA/OSA compliance costs ongoing. State sales tax / marketplace facilitator fully embedded — no longer a delta.

**Macro**: Consumer discretionary mixed; resale tends to be counter-cyclical (recession-friendly). Net setup: mixed-to-modestly-favorable for the resale cohort.
